Argus Education Next — Season 5: forging the future of education

Argus Education Next

A platform for education’s next chapter

Argus Education Next aims to create a collaborative platform to discuss innovation, policy, and growth in education.

The initiative also recognizes institutions that have demonstrated excellence in academics, infrastructure, innovation, and social impact.

The journey so far

Conclave Legacy

From the first dialogue to today — four milestones that shaped how we celebrate learning, leadership, and impact.

  1. 2022

    Season 1

    The inaugural conclave — bringing educators and policymakers together to frame the agenda for educational excellence.

  2. 2023

    Season 2

    Deeper sector participation — spotlighting innovation on campus and in classrooms across regions.

  3. 2024

    Season 3

    Expanded recognition — honouring institutions for infrastructure, inclusion, and learner outcomes.

  4. 2025

    Season 4

    Momentum toward Season 5 — scaling public engagement and jury-led evaluation for the national awards platform.
